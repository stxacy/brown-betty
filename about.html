About Us

Who We Are

More Than a Meal,
It's Family

Brown Betty started the way the best things do — around a kitchen table, with recipes passed down through generations. We bring the warmth, spice, and soul of authentic Caribbean home cooking to South Richmond Hill, serving a community that knows good food.

Every dish is made fresh daily, seasoned properly, and served with the kind of care that only comes from cooking for people you love. From our legendary Chicken N Ruff to slow-cooked lamb curry and hand-rolled roti, there's always something here worth coming back for.

We're proud to serve our neighbourhood and proud to call South Richmond Hill home. Brown Betty isn't just a restaurant — it's a gathering place, a piece of the islands, right here in Queens.
